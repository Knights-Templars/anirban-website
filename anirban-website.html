ABOUT

Currently

I am a postdoctoral researcher working on spectral modeling of thermonuclear supernovae, using radiative transfer codes such as TARDIS. I am currently interested in non-thermal processes and late time energy deposition in supernovae. I am also interested in exotic stellar explosions which defy known explosion models.

RESEARCH

Research Interests

Nebular-phase spectral modeling

One-zone and multi-zone NLTE codes for the late-time spectra of Type Ia supernovae, built to replace legacy nebular codes.

Peculiar thermonuclear explosions

Observing not so normal thermonuclear explosions and understanding how they can explode.

Faint but visible

Looking into LSST data to find faint emission from explosions.
